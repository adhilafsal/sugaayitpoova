# SUGGAYITT POVAA – DISCOVER & LEARN VERIFIED IMAGE DATABASE MANIFEST

This manifest provides the comprehensive, auditable record of all verified photographic and historical assets used in the **Discover & Learn** educational portal.

### Strict Verification Standards:
1. **Zero Collision Policy**: No travel package destination images from `images/places/` are reused for mountaineers, peaks, or historical monuments.
2. **Authenticity Guarantee**: Every individual mountaineer displays their actual verified photograph; every peak displays its own mountain face; every historical site displays its authentic monument.
3. **Structured Storage**: Images are partitioned into isolated directories:
   - `images/discover/mountaineers/` (13 authentic portraits)
   - `images/discover/peaks/` (21 authentic eight-thousander & continental summit photos)
   - `images/discover/history/` (38 authentic monument & heritage photos)
   - `images/discover/knowledge/`
